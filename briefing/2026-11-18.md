# GTA VI briefing — 2026-11-18

## The five that matter

1. **PS5 firmware 26.07-14.23.00 is now mandatory to play GTA VI** (18 Nov)
   Sony's support account says the console update must be installed before the game will run — it is about 1.3 GB, and PS5 owners who skip it will be stopped at the title screen on launch night.
   https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-requires-new-ps5-firmware-update-to-play

2. **The rolling launch starts today — unlock is midnight local** (18 Nov)
   New Zealand goes first at 11:00 UTC (13:00 EET); the UAE unlocks at 8pm UTC, Saudi Arabia and Turkey at 9pm UTC, and the Levant at midnight local. If you pre-loaded you are ready — if not, start the download now.
   https://www.pcgamer.com/games/live/news/grand-theft-auto-6-live-launch-coverage/

3. **Lucia's actor is officially confirmed: Manni L. Perez** (17 Nov)
   The Game Awards nominations named Perez for Best Performance as Lucia — the first official confirmation of the cast — and GTA VI led the show with eight nominations including Game of the Year, even though it is not out yet.
   https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/lucia-actor-confirmed-as-gta-6-sweeps-the-game-awards-nominations/

4. **A fake "GTA VI delayed to 2027" Rockstar letter is circulating — it is false** (17 Nov)
   Lead Stories found no such announcement on Rockstar's site and traced the post to an account that tagged it "ragebait." The game still launches tomorrow.
   https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2026/11/fact-check-fake-rockstar-games-letter-says-gtavi-release-date-pushed-back-2027.html

5. **Pre-orders are at about 15.9 million, a record** (17 Nov)
   Sensor Tower estimates 15.88 million digital pre-orders worth $1.54 billion across PS5 and Xbox, so expect spoilers everywhere and a very busy launch window.
   https://finance.yahoo.com/media-advertising/articles/gta-6-preorders-hit-estimated-215511423.html
