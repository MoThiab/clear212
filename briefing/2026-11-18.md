# GTA VI briefing — 2026-11-18

_Updated 16:08 EET. The review embargo lifted at 11:00 UTC, after the morning run, so this replaces the 06:16 version._

## The five that matter

1. **The first reviews are in: 99 on Metacritic** (18 Nov)
   Critics got the game early and the aggregate opened at 99 — tied with Ocarina of Time as the best-reviewed game ever — with 100% of critics recommending it on OpenCritic; IGN, Eurogamer and The Guardian gave it top marks, and the story runs about 50 to 60 hours.
   https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cw8r75xm8v4po

2. **GTA+ now works inside GTA VI's single-player** (18 Nov)
   Rockstar has extended the $8-a-month subscription into the story mode: members get a free '90 Annis Venturas Blvd Elegy and a garage after Chapter 3, transit upgrades, 10% shop discounts and 10% better car sell prices — and the free month that comes with a digital pre-order auto-renews, so cancel it if you do not want to pay.
   https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-gta-subscription-includes-a-free-supercar-discounts-and-more/

3. **The rolling launch has started — New Zealand is already playing** (18 Nov)
   The game unlocked at 11:00 UTC in New Zealand and rolls westward at midnight local time, so the UAE unlocks at 8pm UTC, Saudi Arabia and Turkey at 9pm UTC, and the US at 9pm PT / midnight ET — expect spoilers online until your own clock strikes midnight.
   https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c60rkg21dl22t

4. **PS5 owners must install firmware 26.07-14.23.00 first** (18 Nov)
   PlayStation Support says the roughly 1.3 GB system update is required before GTA VI will start, so install it tonight from Settings, System, System Software Update instead of at midnight.
   https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-requires-new-ps5-firmware-update-to-play

5. **Rockstar explains why there is no 40fps mode** (18 Nov)
   The game targets 30fps on every console with no performance or balanced option; technology SVP Jonathon Ashcroft says the world's complexity leaves only 33ms per frame, so do not buy a PS5 Pro expecting more frames — it adds upscaling and ray-traced shadows, not fps.
   https://www.techradar.com/gaming/why-no-40fps-mode-for-gta-6-rockstar-says-the-complexity-of-simulating-and-rendering-our-world-requires-it-to-carefully-balance-how-we-spend-every-millisecond-of-both-the-central-processor-and-the-graphics-processor
