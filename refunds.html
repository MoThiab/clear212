Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Where the site is free

Everything on Clear212 is currently free: the countdown, the answers, the progress tracker. There is nothing to refund because nothing is sold here today.

2. If we sell a digital product

If we start selling a digital product — a checklist, a planner, a guide — this is the policy that applies:

  • 14-day window. You have 14 days from the date of purchase to ask for a refund.
  • No questions asked. One email is enough. We do not ask for a reason, though you are welcome to give one.
  • Paid back the same way. Refunds go back to the payment method used, within 10 business days of the request.
  • Delivered instantly. Because a digital file is delivered the moment you pay, the refund window exists in place of a "change of mind" period. Once a refund is issued, your access to the file ends.

3. Affiliate purchases are not our sale

When you follow a link on the answers page and buy from a retailer such as Amazon, the contract is between you and that retailer. Any return, refund, delivery problem or warranty claim is handled by them under their own policy — we have no access to your order and cannot process a refund for it.

4. Email updates can be cancelled

Subscribing to launch updates is free and can be cancelled at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email. There is nothing to refund.

5. How to ask

Email hello@mo-automation.com with the subject line Refund and, if it is about a purchase, the date and the email address you used. We reply within 3 business days.
