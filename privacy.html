Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Clear212 is a fan-made website about Grand Theft Auto VI. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, what stays on your own device, and how to contact us. It is written in plain language on purpose.

The short version: we do not run accounts, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you around the web. The only things stored are the ones your own browser keeps for you, plus cookies set by the shops you choose to buy from after following one of our links.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We have no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not ask for your name, address or payment details.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone — because we do not hold any to sell.

2. Data stored on your device

The countdown page and the progress tracker save your ticks and checklist progress using your browser's local storage. That data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is never transmitted to us or to anyone else;
  • can be deleted at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser, or using the reset control on the tracker page.

3. Cookies and affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to retailers such as Amazon. When you click one of those links, the retailer sets its own cookie on your device so it can credit the sale. That cookie is set by the retailer, not by us, and it is governed by the retailer's own privacy policy.

We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies ourselves.

4. Email updates

If the site offers an email list, subscribing stores your email address with our email provider so we can send launch-week updates. You can unsubscribe from any email in one click. We use the address only for the updates you asked for.

5. Analytics

We do not currently use third-party analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated to name the provider and the data collected before it goes live.

6. Third-party services

  • Hosting provider — stores the files that make up this site. Standard server logs may record your IP address and browser type.
  • Email provider — only if the email list is active.
  • Retailers — Amazon and any other shop linked from the answers page, whose own policies apply once you leave this site.

7. Children

This site is about a mature-rated video game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Material changes will be flagged on the countdown page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete anything we hold about you: contact page — hello@mo-automation.com.
