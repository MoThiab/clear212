# GTA VI Caption Kit

Deliverable 3 of the launch plan. Everything here is text — nothing to build.

---

## 1. The reusable prompt

Paste this into any model, once a day, with the clip details filled in.

```
Write the text for a clip I am posting on X.

My details:
What happens in the clip: [two sentences]
Who it is for: [new players, or long-time fans]

Rules: no hashtags, no all caps, nothing the clip does not show.
Give me five opening lines under 12 words each, then one caption of two
sentences. Finish with the opening line you would post and one reason.
```

Why the rules matter: X's ranking rewards watch time and replies. A specific,
visual first line earns the tap; hashtags and all-caps earn the spam filter.

---

## 2. Worked example

**Clip:** the first ten minutes in Vice City. Driving the neon strip at night,
no mission started yet.
**Audience:** new players.

Five opening lines, each under 12 words:

1. First ten minutes in Vice City. No mission, no plan.
2. The neon strip at night is doing too much.
3. I have been driving in circles for ten minutes.
4. Vice City looks better than the trailer. That is it.
5. No missions yet. Just the city and the radio.

Caption, two sentences:

> First ten minutes in Vice City and I have done nothing but drive and stare.
> New players: the city is the tutorial.

**Pick: line 1.** It sets a scene and a promise in ten words, and "no mission,
no plan" makes the viewer want to know what happens next. Lines 2 and 4 are
compliments — they earn likes, not replays.

## 3. How X clipping actually works (checked Nov 2026)

- X's built-in clipping tool rolled out on 9–10 November 2026. It works on
  videos **2 minutes or longer**, and the clip links back to the original post.
- Both the clip poster and the original creator can earn from a clip — but only
  if both are enrolled in **Original Content Rewards**.
- Keep a clip under about 60 seconds and let motion start immediately.
- Put your opening line in the **post text**, not burned into the video.
- Post natively on X. Linking out to another platform kills both reach and the
  clipping credit.

---

## 4. Eligibility reality — do not build the plan on this

X Original Content Rewards requires, **at the time you apply**:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Verified followers | 500 (verified accounts, not your total follower count) |
| Impressions | 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days |
| Subscription | Active X Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business |
| Age / country | 18+, in a country where the program is available |
| Standing | Good standing, no repeated monetization-guideline strikes |

X publishes no revenue share. Treat X as a **traffic source** that feeds
`answers.html`, not as a business on its own. If the account is not yet
eligible, the clips still have a job: they are the top of the funnel.

## 5. The honest earning math

Affiliate rates as of November 2026 (Amazon Associates; check your own network):

| Product | Rate | On a typical price | You earn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video games | ~1% | $79.99 | $0.80 |
| Gaming peripherals | 1–4% | $69.99 controller | $0.70–2.80 |
| Everything else (unlisted) | ~4% | $180 2TB SSD | $7.20 |

- Cookie window is **1 day**. The link must be clicked the day of the purchase.
- **Storage is the only purchase almost every GTA VI buyer must make in launch
  week** — the preload is 212 GB. That is the affiliate product to optimise for,
  and it is why the storage answer on `answers.html` carries the first link.
- Do not bother linking to the game itself: 1% on an $80 purchase is $0.80.
- A realistic target for launch week is a few hundred dollars, not a salary.
  Scale comes from search traffic over the following months, not from launch
  night itself.

1. **Morning** — run the briefing prompt in `briefing/`, post the three drafts.

3. Every post and clip points back to `answers.html`.
